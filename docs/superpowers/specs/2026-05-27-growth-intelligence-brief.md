# Product Specifications: Growth Intelligence Module

## 1. Keyword Intelligence & Discovery

### The Problem
You don't know which keywords are driving installs — or which ones you're missing.

### Solution
Discover opportunities that drive installs and find/capture high-value keywords before competitors do.

### Core Features
*   **Daily Ranking Snapshots:** Track performance across 1,200+ keywords.
*   **Historical Trend Charts:** Visual timelines to see what changed and exactly when.
*   **Keyword Gap Discovery:** Automatically identify high-value terms you already rank for but haven't explicitly optimized in your metadata.

---

## 2. Competitor Intelligence & Monitoring

### The Problem
Competitors are optimizing their listings, and you only find out after your installs drop.

### Solution
See who is stealing your visibility and why, allowing you to react to competitor moves in hours, not weeks.

### Core Features
*   **Automatic Competitor Discovery:** Identify rivals dynamically based on keyword overlap.
*   **Listing Change Detection:** Real-time tracking of modifications to titles, descriptions, and screenshots.
*   **Pricing Change Alerts:** Instant notifications featuring side-by-side "before and after" pricing values.

---

## 3. Optimization Impact & Experimentation

### The Problem
You update your listing and have no idea if the changes helped or hurt your rankings.

### Solution
Understand which optimizations actually move rankings, turning every listing change into a measurable experiment.

### Core Features
*   **Pre/Post-Change Position Data:** Granular before/after ranking metrics for every single listing modification.
*   **Universal Tracking:** Works seamlessly for both your own apps and your competitors' listings.
*   **Trend Correlation:** Directly correlate metadata updates with install velocity and trends.

---

## 4. Review & Sentiment Intelligence

### The Problem
You don't know how user reviews affect your search rankings or when to act.

### Solution
Track how review velocity shapes your visibility and turn your review strategy into a competitive ranking advantage.

### Core Features
*   **Review Velocity Tracking:** Monitor review frequency and volume across your app and major competitors.
*   **Review-to-Rank Correlation:** Analyze and visualize the 7-day trailing impact of review trends on keyword positions.
*   **Rating Trend Monitoring:** Early warning systems to flag sudden drops in ratings before they impact organic reach.
